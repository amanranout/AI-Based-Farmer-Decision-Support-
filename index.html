Frequently Asked Questions ❓

❓ Is this app free to use?
Yes! SmartFarm AI is completely free for farmers. Government supported initiative.
❓ Does it work offline?
Yes, the app works in low connectivity areas. Syncs when internet is available.
❓ Do I need special equipment?
Optional IoT sensors enhance accuracy, but app works with manual data entry too.

100% secure. Data encrypted & only used for your farming decisions.
