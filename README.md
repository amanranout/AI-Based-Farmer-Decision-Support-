## 📌 Overview

**AgriSense AI** is an intelligent farming platform that empowers farmers with data-driven decisions across the entire agricultural lifecycle — from **crop selection to market selling**.

It integrates **Artificial Intelligence, IoT, and real-time analytics** to improve productivity, reduce risks, and maximize profits.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement

Farmers struggle with:

- ❌ Incorrect crop selection  
- ❌ Unpredictable weather conditions  
- ❌ Inefficient irrigation  
- ❌ Pest & disease attacks  
- ❌ Low profits due to middlemen  

👉 **Impact:** Low yield, high loss, and unstable income

---

## 💡 Solution

A unified smart platform that provides:

- 📱 End-to-end farming assistance  
- 🧠 AI-powered decision making  
- 🌐 Real-time insights  

> **“One Smart App for Complete Farming Decisions”**

---

## ⚙️ Core Features

### 🌱 Smart Crop Recommendation
- AI suggests best crop based on soil + climate data

### 💧 Smart Irrigation System
- IoT sensors monitor soil moisture  
- Automated irrigation decisions  

### 🌦️ Weather Intelligence
- Real-time weather tracking  
- Alerts for extreme conditions  

### 🐛 Disease & Pest Detection
- Image-based AI detection (CNN)  
- Instant treatment suggestions  

### 📊 Yield Prediction
- Forecast crop production using ML models  

### 💰 Smart Market Insights
- Live mandi prices  
- Future price prediction  

### 🏛️ Government Scheme Integration
- PM-KISAN, Fasal Bima & more
